Banaba Native Chicken

Known as the original Filipino fighting bird, the Banaba chicken originates from Batangas (Southern Luzon) and is valued as a hardy gamecock, layer, and broiler.

5 Months Old

Darag Panay Chicken

Native to Panay Island, the Darag chicken is a popular heritage breed prized for its lean, smoky, and flavorful meat—ideal for Inasal and Tinola.

Bolinao Pangasinan Chicken

Hailing from Pangasinan, the Bolinao chicken is an efficient free-range breed suited for both meat and egg production.

Authentic Provenance

Raised with Care. Rooted in Home.

Our pastures are located in the peaceful rolling countryside of Luzon, where native and heritage breeds are cared for under natural tropical sunlight. We honor centuries-old Filipino farming practices combined with modern sanitation and feed management.

Whether you are building a small homestead flock or expanding a commercial native egg supply, each bird is selected for vibrant vitality, authentic genetics, and calm temperament.
